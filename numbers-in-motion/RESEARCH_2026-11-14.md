**Retention curve of the military long video (65 views, the only one with a readable curve):**
115% at 0:00 → ~52% at 0:15 → ~41% at 0:20 → ~35% at 0:30 → **flat 25–28% from 0:35 to 2:30** →
bump to ~36% around 2:35 (people scrubbing to the payoff) → falls away on the end card.
- The opening loses about 6 viewers in 10 inside 20 seconds. A healthy video still has ~60% at 0:30.
- Whoever survives the opening stays to the end. The middle is not the leak.
- If that plateau held on a longer video, a 7-minute video would earn roughly 2:00 per view against 0:50 now.
  That is an assumption drawn from 65 views, not a result.

## 3. What the winners share
1. **A stake for the viewer.** Their own country, their own phone, their own band.
2. **An outcome nobody can guess.** Ten different rules, or a rivalry that flips.
3. **Structure you can see.** Rounds or eras, with a HUD that says how far along we are.
4. **6–17 minutes.** Nothing under 5 minutes appears among the hits.
5. **Topics with fans.** Brands, music, sport, or "every country on Earth". Dry indicators lose, even on their channels.
6. **Series.** The same game repeated with a new rule set, uploaded often.
7. **A low hit rate, accepted.** Roughly one video in four to ten breaks out.

## 4. Our presentation against theirs
Keep: the paper/sticker look, the flag faces (the winners use the same "country as a character" idea), legibility on a phone,
verified data with a named source.

Missing:
- **No stake.** 10–40 countries on screen; most viewers' countries are absent.
- **No visible structure.** No rounds, no HUD, no escalation. "Round 7/15" in small grey text is the whole of it.
- **A predictable result.** Our knockout raises one threshold on one statistic, so it is a sorted list revealed slowly.
- **Dead screen.** The military race leaves the right half empty for three minutes, and frames from 1990, 2000, 2013 and 2024
  look the same: one bar across the screen, nine short ones.
- **The opening.** A static question over a chart. The payoff sits at 2:35.
- **Sound.** A procedural loop, where the hits use cinematic tracks and effects on every event.
- **Packaging.** Descriptive titles and dark, wordy thumbnails → 1.0–2.4% CTR.
- **Length.** 0:35–2:59. Below every hit in the niche.

## 5. Platform facts
- **YPP.** From 1 Feb 2027 new applicants need 1,000 subscribers plus 8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days, or 20M qualified
  Shorts views in 90 days (was 4,000 hours / 10M). The 500-subscriber fan-funding tier is unchanged. Announced 10 Aug 2026.
  Whether an application filed before 1 Feb is judged on the old bar is implied but not confirmed.
  https://ppc.land/new-youtube-creators-face-8-000-hour-bar-for-ad-revenue-from-february-2027/
- **Watch hours** come only from public long-form videos and archived live streams.
  https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/watch-time-vs-average-view-duration
- **Monetization policy ("inauthentic content", formerly "repetitious").** Not eligible: content that looks made from a template
  with low educational value, templated storylines, slideshows or scrolling text without narrative or commentary.
  Allowed: a series in a fixed format where each video has a distinct storyline, focus or concept.
  https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
  Global Stats' About page says outright that its videos are "not auto generated" and points to a making-of trailer.
- **Mid-roll ads** need a video of at least 8 minutes. https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-8-minute-mid-roll-ads/
- **CTR.** Most channels average 4–6%; consistently under 2% means the packaging is failing.
  https://ytgrowth.io/blog/youtube-ctr · https://humbleandbrag.com/blog/new-youtube-channel-average-views
- **Retention.** About 60% still watching at 0:30 is the healthy mark. https://vidiq.com/blog/post/increase-audience-retention-youtube/

## 6. Recommendation (pending Ahmed)
**Make the long video a game, 6–8 minutes: "Last Country Standing 2.0".**
- All ~195 countries start on screen. 8–10 rounds. Each round is a different rule built on a real, sourced statistic.
- HUD: round n/10, countries left, big names still alive. A suspense check on the countries people care about.
- Cold open on the most surprising elimination. Rules card in under 15 seconds. Crown for the survivor.
- Title built on the viewer ("Your country is OUT if…", "Only 1 survives"). Bright thumbnail, four words at most.
- One Short per video: the best round, cut as a teaser. Still one Short per day.
- Judge after six videos, not one: CTR ≥ 4%, ≥ 55% still watching at 0:30, AVD ≥ 2:30.
- Avoid rules about war, religion or politics.

Second line, later: an "epic span" race with era captions and a story panel on a topic with fans and solid data
(Olympic medals 1896–2024, World Cup goals). Not recommended: more 3-minute races on dry indicators.

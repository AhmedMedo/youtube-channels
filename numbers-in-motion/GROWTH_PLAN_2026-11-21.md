# Numbers in Motion — Growth plan (approved by Ahmed, 21 Nov 2026)

Goal: apply for the YouTube Partner Program before 31 Jan 2027 (1,000 subscribers + 4,000 qualified long-form watch
hours; the hour bar is expected to double for applications from 1 Feb 2027). Ahmed's wish is 1–2 months; that needs a
breakout episode and cannot be promised.

## The math (21 Nov)
- Ep 1 after 7 days: 1,100 views → 44.7 h → +3 subscribers (0.3%). Niche competitors convert at about the same 0.3%.
- 4,000 h ≈ 100K long-form views at our watch time. 1,000 subs ≈ 350K views at 0.3%.
- Subscribers are the bottleneck. At 1% conversion, ~100K views covers both thresholds.

## Decisions
1. **Three episodes a week: Saturday, Tuesday, Thursday, 22:00 Cairo.** Each is a distinct concept (new rules, data, winner).
2. **Ep 4 topic: Ahmed said yes to both options without choosing; default is US States (27% of viewers are in the US),
   then Flags.** Later: continent editions (Europe, Africa, Asia), football, "you chose the rules" from comments.

## Per-episode checklist (from ep 4)
- Motion pass on (default), subscribe button after the crown, "Ep N" in the title.
- **Title = the episode's surprise**, not the template ("The USA Is OUT in Round 1?! …"). Thumbnail in a different
  colour/edition look each time. Run Studio's A/B thumbnail test with 3 variants.
- **Next-episode teaser** on the end card ("Next: Flag Edition, Saturday").
- **Three Shorts**, each a different round with different big countries, one per day, all with Related video → the long.
  Plus a **winner Short** two days after the episode.
- **Translated title + description**: Arabic, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Hindi (Studio → Subtitles).
- Pinned question comment; reply to every comment in the first day (Claude drafts, Ahmed approves).
- Files to `~/Documents/Number of Motions/<date> - <title>/`.

## Off YouTube
- Same Shorts to TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels. Ahmed creates the accounts; Claude prepares files and captions.
- Channel page: trailer for non-subscribers = Ep 1, banner with the schedule.

## Not doing
- Paid Promote / ads: YouTube states watch hours and subscribers from ad campaigns do not count toward YPP.
- Sub-for-sub, bought views, spam posting.

## Weekly review (Saturdays)
Subscribers per 1,000 views (target 10), long-form CTR (target ≥ 5%), average view duration (target ≥ 2:30),
watch hours and subscribers against the 4,000 / 1,000 line.

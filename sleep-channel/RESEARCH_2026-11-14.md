## 3. What the winners share
1. **Length for the night.** 8–12 hours is the norm in black-screen sleep. One overnight listener is worth one whole video.
2. **Darkness is the feature.** Black screen from the start, or "black screen after 30 min".
3. **The title names the need, then the sound.** "Quiet Your Racing Mind", "If You Wake Up at 3AM, Play This".
4. **A sound you can name in two words.** Low cello. Copper rain. Rain on a canvas tent. Not "ambient music".
5. **A thumbnail that is type, not a picture.** Two to four large words on a dark frame, plus hours and "black screen".
6. **Rain is where new channels get in.** Both breakouts younger than six months are rain. On the same young channel,
   abstract synthetic sounds got 1–7K where named rains got 30–180K.
7. **Proof that a person made it.** Behind-the-scenes clips, a founder story, "NO AI", "own recordings".
8. **Volume and patience.** 36–677 videos. The young channels' first month sat at 1–10K per video.

## 4. Our presentation against theirs
Keep: the sleeping-cloud mark (a rain cloud suits a rain channel), original audio, the dim palette, clean encoding.

Missing or working against us:
- **Length.** 1–3 hours. The hours badge on our thumbnail ("2 HOURS") advertises the one thing we are short of.
- **The animated scene.** It took most of the render time and it is what listeners least need: they want the screen dark.
  We then make them choose between two uploads (scene / black screen), which splits the views.
- **The look.** Flat cartoon with a blushing mascot. Next to black-and-white type thumbnails it reads as a children's
  lullaby channel; the cartoon-character thumbnail in 2a (This Little Piggy) belongs to a baby-sleep channel. This is a
  judgement, not a measurement — the thumbnail A/B test is how we check it.
- **The sound has no name.** "Calm ambient music" is the most crowded and least specific promise in the niche
  (see 2b: that query is won by jazz, folk and lofi with rich artwork).
- **Titles.** Keyword-first but generic: "Deep Sleep Music 🌙 3 Hours of Calm Ambient Music to Fall Asleep Fast". No need, no named sound.
- **Three uploads per episode.** Main + black screen + Short. The Short brings views that do not count; the black screen competes with the main.

## 5. Platform facts
- **YPP.** From 1 Feb 2027 new applicants need 1,000 subscribers plus **8,000** qualified watch hours in 365 days (was 4,000).
  Announced 10 Aug 2026. Qualified hours = public long-form + archived live streams; Shorts, private/unlisted and
  un-archived live streams do not count.
  https://ppc.land/shorts-watched-as-ads-do-not-count-toward-youtubes-20-million-view-bar/
  8,000 h a year is 21.9 h a day: about three or four people sleeping through an 8-hour video every night
  (eleven a night if the video is 2 hours).
- **Inauthentic content** (formerly "repetitious"): mass-produced or repetitive content, where videos feel interchangeable,
  is not monetizable, and the policy is applied to the channel as a whole. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
- **AI / altered-content disclosure.** YouTube's list of what must be disclosed now includes music that is the main focus
  of a video, with "AI generated music" as an example; animation and clearly unrealistic content are exempt.
  https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491 — Third-party summaries of YouTube's position say the label does not by
  itself limit reach or monetization. https://jackrighteous.com/en-us/blogs/ai-music-distribution-guide/youtube-ai-policy-for-suno-creators
  **Our audio is synthesized by our own code, written with AI. We currently answer "No". That is a grey area and the
  safer answer is "Yes". Ahmed's call (section 7).**
- **"No ads" claims.** YouTube may run ads on videos from channels outside YPP, and such channels have no ad controls.
  We cannot promise "No Ads" or "No mid-roll ads" until we are in YPP and can switch mid-rolls off.
  https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/357996/youtube-puts-ads-on-non-partner-videos-sans-payme.html
- **Live streams.** Hours count only when the stream is archived as a public video; streams over 12 hours may not be archived.
  A later lever, not a first one. https://blog.livereacting.com/youtube-monetization-requirements-2026-checklist-fastest-path-to-4-000-watch-hours/
- **Saturation.** An August 2026 audit of the niche found 19 of 120 sampled videos were 10x outliers, 11 of them from channels
  under 1,000 subscribers; in a 12-channel sample, 10 had under 5,000 subscribers, average views per video ran from 17 to
  91,896, and the channel names were close to interchangeable. Small channels do break out; most clones get nothing.
  https://outlierkit.com/resources/sleep-ambient-niche-audit/

## 6. Risks and unknowns
- **Can our code make rain that passes for rain?** The current rain bed is filtered noise. The winners say "real recordings".
  This has to pass Ahmed's ears on a two-minute sample before anything ships. If it fails, the choices are licensed/CC0
  recordings (a PLAYBOOK change) or staying music-led.
- **We must never say "real rain" or "recorded".** "Rain soundscape, made in our studio, never loops" is true.
- **Our own evidence is thin.** 111 long views. The market evidence in section 2 carries most of the weight.
- **Cartoon look and made-for-kids.** The channel is set to not-for-kids. A cute mascot on every thumbnail invites the wrong neighbours.

## 7. Recommendation (pending Ahmed)

**One upload per episode: 8 hours, a 10–15 second title card with the cloud, then black. A sound with a name.
A title that starts with the need. A dark thumbnail that is mostly type.**

- **Test two families side by side, one of each every Saturday for three Saturdays:**
  - A. *Named rain*, no music — a new rain engine with surfaces (tin roof, canvas tent, window glass, leaves), distant thunder optional.
  - B. *Driftlight music*, darker and steadier (no bells after the first minutes), with or without light rain.
  Same length, same black screen, same title and thumbnail system, so the only difference is the sound.
- **Title pattern:** `<need> — <named sound> | Black Screen | 8 Hours`, e.g. "Quiet a Racing Mind — Soft Rain on a Tin Roof | Black Screen | 8 Hours".
  No medical promises ("cures insomnia", "99%").
- **Thumbnail:** two styles through Studio's Test & Compare on every video — (1) dark rain texture + the sound's name in
  spaced capitals + "BLACK SCREEN · 8 HOURS" + small cloud mark; (2) plain black with large white type.
- **Audio:** generated continuously with layers of unequal length, so nothing repeats inside 8 hours. Say so in the description.
- **Pause Shorts, the Pinterest pin and the separate black-screen upload** during the test. Ahmed's work drops from six uploads a week to two.
- **Delivery:** black video is tiny, so 8 h at ~104 kbps AAC ≈ 357 MiB fits the 400 MiB limit.
- **Later, once a family wins:** a short "how Driftlight is made" video and an About-page story (authorship evidence for the YPP review).

## 8. How we judge it
One number: **watch hours per 1,000 impressions** (it is CTR × time watched, and it is what YouTube rewards with more impressions).
- Baseline: **1.5 h per 1,000 impressions, 1.4% CTR.**
- Read each video at 10 days. First verdict after six videos (three Saturdays).
- A family that reaches **≥ 3% CTR and ≥ 5 h per 1,000 impressions** on two of its three videos becomes the channel's main line.
- If neither family doubles the baseline after six videos, the sound is not convincing listeners: stop and rethink the source
  (recordings) before making more.

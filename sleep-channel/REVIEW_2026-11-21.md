## Three findings
1. **We only exist on upload days.** 81% of all impressions (7,595 of 9,334) and 74% of watch hours landed on
   the four upload days. YouTube gives each new video a test burst (700–4,000 impressions), then stops. Over the
   last five days the channel averaged 88 impressions and 6 views a day. The back catalogue earns nothing on its own.
2. **The test fails at the hold, not the click.** CTR in the working thumbnail family is 2.7–3.0%. Then the
   retention curve falls off a cliff in the first two minutes on every long video (ep004: AVD 4:39 of 3 h, 2%).
   ep004's audience is 100% new viewers, 0% returning. People click, hear it, leave. That is a verdict on the
   sound, and it is the signal YouTube uses to stop the test.
3. **v3 fixed the packaging, and the packaging was not the difference.** See the comparison below: the channels
   that are stuck use the same 8-hour black-screen package we just adopted.

Exceptions worth knowing: ep003 black screen holds 11.7 h per 1,000 impressions (AVD 12:31) on 23 views, and
18–20 Nov show 3.9 hours from 23 views, mostly direct/playlist. A handful of long listens — possibly real
returning listeners, possibly Ahmed's own playback. Too few to build on.

## A correction to the case for 8 hours
On 18 Nov, ep004 read 11.2 h and I called length "the lever" (1 h → 1.56, 2 h → 2.58, 3 h → 2.84 h per 1,000
impressions). YouTube has since revised ep004 down to **9.9 h** (views rose 125 → 135). Revised: 1 h → 1.59,
2 h → 2.57, 3 h → **2.42**. The step from 2 h to 3 h is gone. ep004 looked dominant mainly because it received
four times the impressions (4,091), not because each impression was worth more. The 8-hour format still matches
what wins in the niche, but our own data does not prove it, and I overstated it.

## What to do
1. **Next episodes are named rain** from `shared/rain.py` (Ahmed approved the samples by ear on 18 Nov):
   8 h, black screen, one upload — e.g. "Rain on a Tin Roof", "Rain on a Canvas Tent". `build_episode.py`
   needs a rain mode; it only knows the music today. Never say "real" or "recorded".
2. **Open at full sound.** No slow fade-in: the listener decides in seconds. Rain at full level from second 1.
3. **Two uploads a week on fixed days, never two on one day.** Each upload gets its own test burst.
   Hold ep005 for mid-week rather than stacking it on ep006.
4. **Judge each video at day 7 on three numbers:** impressions (was it tested), CTR, watch hours per 1,000
   impressions. Target ≥ 3% and ≥ 5 h. Channel health: impressions on non-upload days (now ~90/day).
5. **Gate: six rain episodes, about three weeks.** If none clears the target and the non-upload-day baseline
   has not moved, the synthesised sound is not convincing listeners: switch to licensed real recordings
   (a PLAYBOOK change) or stop. Do not drift into month four the way the 300-video channels did.

Expect no monetization this side of several months even if it works: 1,000 subscribers at this niche's
0.06–0.4% conversion is 250K–1.6M views.
